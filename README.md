# 🥛 MilkFlow - Smart Milk Delivery & Billing App

**MilkFlow** is a powerful Android app that simplifies the milk delivery process for small-scale vendors and suppliers. It includes smart customer management, automated daily sales, billing, and payment tracking — all backed by Appwrite and Firebase.

---

## 📦 About

MilkFlow is designed for **daily milk delivery businesses** to:

- Add and manage customers (with unique phone numbers).
- Assign products, prices, milk types, and delivery schedules.
- Track daily milk delivery per customer.
- Generate monthly bills automatically.
- Handle payments and pending balances.
- Receive push notifications via Firebase.
- Operate on a secure backend powered by Appwrite.

## ✨ Core Features

### 👥 Customer Management
- Add customers with **unique numbers**.
- Set:
  - **Start date**
  - **Vacation mode**
  - **Active/inactive status**
- Assign product, price, delivery time, and milk type (cow, buffalo, both).

### 📈 Daily Milk Sales
- Record sales with default and updated quantity.
- Select customers and update sales instantly.
- Generate daily sale report.

### 🧾 Monthly Billing
- Auto-generate monthly bills based on sales data.
- View total, paid, and pending amounts.
- Accept payments and update records.

### 💳 Payments
- Multiple payment modes supported: **Cash, UPI, Online**.
- Store transaction date, remarks, and auto-update pending balance.

### 🔔 Notifications
- Push notifications powered by **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)**.
